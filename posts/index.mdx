# Home

## Motivation

Greetings.
I'm Rob.

I'm interested in improving NPC behaviour.<SideNote>
A "Non-Player Character" (NPC) is a video game character not controlled by the Player.</SideNote>
{/* This involves programming the inhabitants of virtual worlds. */}
{/* However, the current resurgence of interest in robots and AI */}
{/* apparently does not extend to the ones we've already invited into our homes. */}

Why?
Well, video game NPCs are usually rigid and predictable.
They're strongly tied into the specifics of the game,
viewed as one of many systems designed to entertain the Player.
How to remedy this situation?
We begin with motivating questions.

- Is NPC behaviour meaningful outside a video game?
- Can we specify NPC behaviour interactively?
- Can a human Game Master act indistinguishably from many humans playing individual parts?

We address the first two questions in the next section,
and the final one in our closing remarks.

## Backdrop

The 3D worlds on this site implement Robert Pearce's [Starship Geomorphs](http://travellerrpgblog.blogspot.com/2020/07/starship-geomorphs-20.html)
i.e.
architectural drawings of starships blocks, connectable along their edges. 

We use Eric Smith's [PNGs](https://ericbsmith.no-ip.org/geomorphs/) to recursively define [SVG symbols](https://github.com/rob-myers/npc-cli-next/tree/main/media/symbol).
Each hull symbol <SideNote>For example [102--hull.svg](/symlink/102--hull.svg)</SideNote> induces a 3D geomorph.
Our [maps](https://github.com/rob-myers/npc-cli-next/tree/main/media/map) amount to possibly edge-sharing rectangles, where each rectangle corresponds to a hull symbol.

For characters we use a basic Blender model, texture-mapped with simplifications of [Minecraft skins](https://github.com/rob-myers/npc-cli-next/tree/main/media/npc).


## Motivation

Our underlying motivations are:

- __NPCs indistinguishable from human-controlled characters.__
  
  A human Game Master makes this tractable.
  Can one human act indistinguishably from many humans playing individual parts?
  <SideNote>It also motivates realtime control systems.</SideNote>

- __Understand [Recast-Detour](https://github.com/recastnavigation/recastnavigation).__
  
  Our NPCs are driven by Recast-Detour.
  We'll analyse this important C++ library in depth.

- __Generic scripting for [three.js](https://www.npmjs.com/package/three).__
  
  We interpret [mvdan-sh](https://github.com/mvdan/sh) syntax trees as JavaScript.
  In production one can run commands.
  In development, shell functions and processes are synced with JS modules.<SideNote>Technically this approach is independent of three.js.</SideNote>
